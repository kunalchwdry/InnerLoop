## 4. AI Assistant

The AI should understand natural language such as:

"Add DSA from 7 PM to 8 PM tomorrow."

"Move my Python session to 9 PM."

"Show me today's tasks."

"Mark DSA as completed."

The AI should NOT directly modify files.

AI → structured command → Python function → data update
